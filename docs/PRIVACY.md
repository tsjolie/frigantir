# Frigantir — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

Frigantir is a client for Frigate, a network video recorder that you run on your own hardware.

**The developer collects no data.** The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting,
and it doesn't send anything to the developer or any third party.

**What the app stores on your device:** the address of your Frigate server and your display preferences
(in the app's settings), and your Frigate password and notification server token (in the iOS Keychain).
Deleting the app deletes them.

**Who the app talks to:**

- **Your Frigate server**, at the address you enter, to show live video, recordings and events.
- **Your notification server**, only if you turn on notifications: the app sends it your device's push
  token, your device name and which cameras and alert types you chose. That server is one you run
  yourself; its operator (you) controls that data.
- **Apple's push notification service** delivers notifications from your notification server to your
  device, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Camera video, audio and events go directly between your device and your servers.
